# LNZ

**Take control of your short-form video feed, using a model of how your brain responds to it.**

LNZ is a Chrome extension that floats a small overlay on TikTok and X, runs three independent AI filters on every video, and skips what you don't want. Settings are locked behind a PIN.

Its **Calm** filter is the novel part. Other filters sort videos into content categories. Calm runs a student model we **distilled from Meta's TRIBE v2 brain-encoding foundation model**. Each second, the student predicts the response of 148 cortical regions from what the video shows and sounds like, then turns that into a live stress score. TRIBE v2 itself needs about 80 seconds on an H100 per 10-second clip; our student does each second in milliseconds, using a model small enough for a browser.

## Built with Kiro: gpu-overwatch

Distilling a brain foundation model meant hours of H100 time, and out of the box Kiro **could not touch a GPU**. So we built **gpu-overwatch**, a Kiro Power that lets Kiro rent, size, smoke-test, launch, watch, budget-guard and tear down Runpod GPUs as if they were local. Every line was generated by Kiro from a spec we wrote, [`docs/overwatch-plan.md`](docs/overwatch-plan.md).

We designed it around how GPU work fails, and wired every part of Kiro to a real job:

- **MCP:** a 20-tool `overwatch` server (`gpu_recommend`, `run_smoke`, `run_launch`, `run_metrics`, `run_restart`, `fleet_reap`, `budget_status`, …) alongside the official Runpod MCP server, in [`gpu-overwatch/mcp.json`](gpu-overwatch/mcp.json).
- **Hooks (8)** make the safety model enforceable, not just advice:
  - `guard-launch` and `guard-runpod` (PreToolUse) block any GPU action that would break the budget or the GPU allowlist, or that comes before a passing smoke test;
  - `audit-log` (PostToolUse) records every GPU action;
  - `start-supervision` (PostToolUse) begins watching a run the moment it launches;
  - `preflight-on-save` re-checks a training config whenever it is saved;
  - `reap-on-stop` and `session-summary` find orphaned pods at the end of a session and report total spend;
  - `fleet-brief` (SessionStart) opens every session with a fleet and budget summary.
- **Agents (2):**
  - `gpu-operator` runs the lifecycle;
  - `gpu-medic` is an on-call engineer that takes the **smallest safe action** on a failing run. It may never raise a budget, delete a volume or kill a kept pod.
- **Workflows (2):**
  - `supervise-run` is a repeat loop: watch, triage, then a post-mortem report;
  - `datagen` fans out teacher inference and QA-grades the samples.
- **Steering (4):** the GPU policy, training contract, datagen and setup rules, shared by the generated code and the live agents.
- **Skills (6):** `setup`, `gpu-sizing`, `launch-run`, `triage-run`, `dataset-fanout` and `teardown`.
- **Training-aware detectors:**
  - NaN loss: restart at half the learning rate;
  - divergence: loss rising for 200 steps;
  - out of memory: halve the micro-batch;
  - stall: no step or near-idle GPU for 10 minutes;
  - preemption: three missed polls;
  - eval regression: worse three times in a row;
  - budget: warn at 80% of a cap, hard-stop at 100%.

  A per-pod sidecar checkpoints every 15 minutes and stops the pod on its own if Kiro's session dies.
- **Guardrails:**
  - spend caps only go down;
  - nothing launches before a passing smoke test;
  - with a real API key, the backend never falls back silently to fake data;
  - run tokens stay out of git and out of the model's context.
- **Proven on real hardware:**
  - the backend talks to the live Runpod REST and pricing APIs using only Python's standard library;
  - it created the 200 GB US-MO-1 network volume, then launched, watched, stopped and terminated H100 80GB pods inside the budget guard.
- **Built for this project:** Kiro also wrote the keyless on-pod TRIBE teacher-queue pipeline ([`pod/`](pod/)):
  - no SSH and no GitHub token on the GPU;
  - a sha256 hard-stop gate on the Llama weights;
  - a split with teacher inference on one GPU and student features on the other.

  gpu-overwatch's caps, H100 allowlist and that two-GPU split shaped the final distillation run in [`pipeline/`](pipeline/).
- **Offline test suite:** about 12,000 lines and 261 tests, all standard library: no network, no GPU, no installs.

Full write-ups: [`docs/GPU_OVERWATCH.md`](docs/GPU_OVERWATCH.md) and [`docs/how-we-used-kiro.md`](docs/how-we-used-kiro.md).

## The Calm model: TRIBE v2 distilled for the browser

TRIBE v2 predicts fMRI responses across the cortex from video, audio and speech, by chaining V-JEPA 2, Llama 3.2 3B, Wav2Vec-BERT and a WhisperX transcript. We ran the full model as a teacher on H100s, then trained a small student to copy it.

### Strengths

- **Small and fast:**
  - frozen MobileCLIP-S1 vision encoder (21.5M params) and EfficientAT mn10_as audio encoder (4.9M);
  - a head under 5M params;
  - about 54 MB of fp16 ONNX in total;
  - about 10 ms per one-second window on an H100.
- **Live by design:** a causal 3-second window updates the score every second as the clip plays. It needs no transcript and no lookahead.
- **A brain map, not a black box:** each second, the student predicts all 148 Destrieux cortical parcels and 6 regions of interest (left/right anterior insula, dACC, mPFC, early auditory, early visual), so the dashboard can show *where* a clip is predicted to drive the brain.
- **Tracks the teacher:** on held-out clips, the correlation with TRIBE is 0.50 mean / 0.54 median across parcels. By region: visual 0.63, auditory 0.50, mPFC 0.40, insula 0.33, dACC 0.22.
- **Fits LNZ's Calm contract:** the stress readout gives `score` in `[0, 1]`, the per-second curve gives `timeline`, and the ROIs feed `regions`. See [`docs/INTEGRATION.md`](docs/INTEGRATION.md).
- **Cheap and self-stopping:** the final run used two H100s, a dead-man switch on each pod, and atomic claim files to share clips. It cost about $10.

### Limits

- **Stress AUROC on TikHarm:** student 0.62, TRIBE regions 0.68. TikHarm's labels are moderation tags that correlate with language and region, not stress ratings; a raw-feature baseline reaches 0.93.
- **Cortex only:** TRIBE predicts no subcortical regions such as the amygdala.
- **English only:** TRIBE's text pathway assumes English.
- **Not wired in yet:** the model is not connected to the extension's live classifier slot. That's the next step.

Full numbers are in [`docs/CALM_MODEL_REPORT.md`](docs/CALM_MODEL_REPORT.md), and the demo clips are broken down in [`brain-demo/DEMO_CLIPS.md`](brain-demo/DEMO_CLIPS.md).

## Run it

### Extension and dashboard (Node 20+)

```bash
npm install
npm run build:ext        # -> extension/dist
npm run dev:analytics    # -> http://localhost:5174 (binds 127.0.0.1 only)
npm test
```

Load the extension: Chrome → `chrome://extensions` → Developer mode → **Load unpacked** → `extension/dist`. Then open <https://www.tiktok.com> or <https://x.com>. The first launch asks you to create a PIN.

Chrome 137+ no longer honours `--load-extension` for branded Chrome. Load unpacked manually, or use Chrome for Testing / Chromium for a scripted launch. The automated fixture run in [`docs/TESTING.md`](docs/TESTING.md) used Chrome for Testing 149.

### Brain demo

Open `brain-demo/live_demo.html` in a browser. Keep it next to `brain-demo/demo/` so it can find the clips.

### gpu-overwatch (Python 3.10+)

```bash
python3 -m pytest -q tests/   # offline: no network, GPU or installs
overwatch doctor              # after `pip install -e .`: environment health check
```

To install the Power in Kiro, add `gpu-overwatch/` as a Power, with `RUNPOD_API_KEY` set in the environment.

### Stage previews (no platform account needed)

Run `npm run dev:analytics`, then open `http://localhost:5174/stage/tiktok.html`. This is a synthetic four-clip TikTok-style stage. The extension injects the **real overlay** into it, with the PIN lock, decision engine and demo classifiers. Every value is labelled **DEMO DATA**, and the clips were generated for review, not taken from TikTok.

The X-style stage is at `http://localhost:5174/stage/x.html?platform=x`. The header switch has four icons: the TikTok stage, the X stage, live tiktok.com and live x.com. The dashboard header has the same switch. `./scripts/demo-stage.sh` builds the extension and opens the stage in Chrome for Testing / Chromium. Set `CHROME_FOR_TESTING` if the browser is elsewhere, and `LNZ_STAGE=x` for the X stage.

### Split-screen demo

- **Left:** Chrome on TikTok or X with LNZ.
- **Right:** a second window on `http://localhost:5174`, on the **Session** or **Day** tab.

The extension posts every video, score and decision to the dashboard. With no extension connected, press **Run demo** (or open `?demo=1`) for a labelled fixture stream. Set the header user with `?user=Your%20Name&role=Researcher`. If the dashboard runs on another machine, forward the port with `ssh -L 5174:localhost:5174 <host>`, and keep the relay local.

The **Day** tab summarises the local day log:
- unique videos watched, and kept / skipped / pending counts;
- average Calm and Learn scores;
- an hourly watch chart and a TikTok/X split;
- Shield flags and the latest videos.

Events are appended to `analytics/.data/events-YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl`, which is gitignored and never uploaded.

## How it behaves

- **Launcher:** a mask, lit cyan when LNZ is on and a grey outline when off. A padlock badge shows lock state, and when LNZ is on the three filter icons sit under it.
- **Heat rail:** with the panel collapsed, Calm, Learn and Shield carry live health rings: green clear, amber pending or uncertain, red would skip.
- **X posts:** the adapter picks the most visible video in a post or X's modal player. The post text, `@handle` and `/status/<id>` become the video identity, and a rejected post advances to the next one with video.
- **Half-screen session:** a 2×2 grid of Calm / Learn / Shield / Rules. The Calm card shows lit lobe tiles (Frontal, Parietal, Temporal, Occipital) plus Limbic / Cerebellum chips.
- **Locked (PROTECTED):** the filters run, and any change opens the PIN dialog. Turning LNZ *on* never needs the PIN; turning it *off* does.
- **Unlocked (EDIT MODE):** playback pauses while you edit. Save & lock applies the change; Cancel restores the last saved config.
- **Decision:** a video is kept only if every enabled filter passes. Unavailable classifiers show as unknown, never as a pass.
- **Feed filtering:** blocked items are removed from TikTok and X feed responses before the site renders them. The fetch/XHR interceptor uses keyword, hashtag, creator and ad rules plus classifier verdicts judged ahead. A DOM safety net catches anything that slips through. See [`docs/FEED_FILTERING.md`](docs/FEED_FILTERING.md).
- **PIN:**
  - stored only as a PBKDF2-SHA256 verifier (310k iterations, random salt);
  - 3 free attempts, then a 30 s back-off that doubles each time;
  - a browser restart always comes back locked.

The PIN is self-control friction. It cannot stop someone removing the extension.

## Privacy

LNZ is a private local build: an unpacked extension, a private repo, a loopback-only dashboard and a gitignored day log. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for the exact boundary. gpu-overwatch keeps run tokens in `.overwatch/state.json`, which is gitignored and listed in `.kiroignore`.

## Demo data

With no models connected, the **Demo** provider returns deterministic fixtures from a hash of the video id. These are labelled **DEMO** everywhere. They are not predictions.
